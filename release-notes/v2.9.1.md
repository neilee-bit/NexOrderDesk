## NEX ORDER DESK 2.9.1

### Fixed
- **Print method box:** with many print types the buttons were squeezed and their text was cut off. Now: 3 columns for up to 6 types, 4 columns for 7-8, and from 9 types the rest open from a **More ▾** button (second level - pick from there, select up to 2 in total). The box is taller on the T-shirt sheet, so the buttons are bigger.
- **Submitted sheet, print and PDF:** the print method is shown only in its title row (big, filling the box) - no small buttons underneath. All order types.
- **No more cut-off text on pill buttons** anywhere in the app: long text shrinks to fit the button.

### Improved
- **Bottoms / Shorts layout:** sizes and special notes in a more compact column on the right; wider pictures and material bar on the left.
- **Bottoms / Shorts colour:** a plain text field (default WHITE) - no swatch or colour picker on these sheets.

### Notes
- Settings, option lists, customers, order types and saved PDFs are kept when you update.
