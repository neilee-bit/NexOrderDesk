## NEX ORDER DESK 2.9.1

### Fixed
- **Option selection rebuilt (all order types):** every option area - print types, the More ▾ list, Additional Custom (bottoms / shorts), Additional Customisation (jackets) - now uses one shared option grid with rectangular option cards. The grid works out how many columns fit from the real names, adds rows automatically and wraps a long name onto two lines - nothing is clipped, hidden or overlapping, the whole card is clickable and selected options are clearly highlighted (tick boxes where several can be chosen). The print-type box is as tall as its options need; specs cards can never cut off their content.
- **Print method box:** with many print types the buttons were squeezed and their text was cut off. Now: 3 columns for up to 6 types, 4 columns for 7-8, and from 9 types the rest open from a **More ▾** button (second level - pick from there, select up to 2 in total). The box is taller on the T-shirt sheet, so the buttons are bigger.
- **Submitted sheet, print and PDF:** the print method is shown only in its title row (big, filling the box) - no small buttons underneath. All order types.
- **No more cut-off text** anywhere on the sheets: long text on buttons shrinks to fit; the help text in the image boxes wraps instead of running out of the box. An automatic check now confirms nothing is cut off on every order type - while editing, after Submit and in print / PDF.

### Improved
- **Bottoms / Shorts layout:** sizes and special notes in a more compact column on the right; wider pictures and material bar on the left.
- **Bottoms / Shorts colour:** a plain text field (default WHITE) - no swatch or colour picker on these sheets.
- **Cut and Print:** uses the same short spec list as Print and Press - Collar Patch, Placket and Piping with tick boxes and colour; Collar Patch and Placket appear on the submitted sheet only when ticked.
- **Printing Method after Submit:** just the chosen method (e.g. FULL SUBLIMATION) in one compact row, the size of the material row, instead of a big box; long names wrap inside the row. The space saved goes to the pictures and specs.
- **Piping after Submit:** the Piping row is left off the submitted sheet / print / PDF when piping is not ticked (all tops order types).
- **Flags sheet:** flag material / colour moved to the top with a bigger, bolder material name; flag sizes are a compact column on the right with big size numbers and every filled size line highlighted.
- **Sleeve Type in two columns:** a sleeve length choice (Short, Long, Short/Long) now sits in front of the sleeve type on T-shirt and other full-spec tops sheets, and prints on the submitted sheet / PDF.

### Notes
- Settings, option lists, customers, order types and saved PDFs are kept when you update.
